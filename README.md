Mod That turns Bot Games into matchmaking games with separate inventory, level and ranks by intercepting GC communication.


> [!NOTE]
> I Cannot fully implement operation due to compatibility issue, instead coins are granted automatically after launching in build that was during exact operation,<br>
> Ex: 23 May 2017 (Operation Hydra). Also missions are not handled in anyway, To get higher coin level there are these requirements:<br>
> Level 0 (Bronze): Launch the game<br>
> Level 1 (Silver): 10 Competetive Wins, 100 Competetive Kills<br>
> Level 2 (Gold): 25 Competetive Wins, 200 Competetive Kills<br>
> Level 3 (Diamond, Introduced in Wildfire): 40 Competetive Wins, 300 Competetive Kills<br>

- Scaleform Builds -
